Color System

These colors were chosen to be bold, to make statements. At the same time, the breadth of this palette is intended to foster versatility and support accessibility.

The color matrix at the bottom of the page is intended to demonstrate all of the potential pairings and which levels of usage are available to specific use cases, as defined by WCAG contrast standards.

All of these colors also have personal importance. Beyond their loud aesthetics, they were derived from experiences traveling in Iceland across several trips. Certain pairings evoke personal memories and can be found in my photography there in various permutations.

Black

HEX: #000000

RGB: 0, 0, 0

Uses: Primary text,borders, and structural elements.

White

HEX: #FFFFFF

RGB: 255, 255, 255

Uses: Page backgrounds, card surfaces, and text on dark backgrounds. This is a neutral foundation to allow the content to lead.

Yellow

HEX: #F5F569

RGB: 245, 245, 105

Uses: Section labels, tags, accent highlights, footer border. It's loud and brings the energy where needed.

Blue

HEX: #33658A

RGB: 51, 101, 138

Uses: Section labels, tags, and cooler accents.

Orange

HEX: #F26419

RGB: 242, 100, 25

Uses: Interactive states (e.g. hovers), primary dividers, primary accent. It's intended to be a note of action rather than a cautionary orange.

Dark Grey

HEX: #5C5750

RGB: 92, 87, 80

Uses: Secondary text, eyebrows, and other UI elements.

Light Grey

HEX: #DDDAD3

RGB: 221, 218, 211

Uses: Component backgrounds, versatility against dark backgrounds.
