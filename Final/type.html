Typography System

The intention of this type system is to present a way to feel loud and bold, but at the same time clean and polished. Fonts were picked to provide a cohesive range of character and personality that doesn't compromise on legibility or accessibility.

As an example of this, Atkinson Hyperlegible was selected to be the primary body text because of its legibility. It was designed by the Braille Institute to have highly distinctive letters and numbers. I first discovered this font used in education building signage, and enjoy the way it feels modern and incredibly readable.

I am planning on introducing a personal logo and wordmark along with this portfolio refresh, and the font choices here will also define the font palette for accompanying wordmark elements.
